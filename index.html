Systems thinking

How I approach engineering problems

Evidence first, verification always, and documentation that improves the next build.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Review expected operation, constraints, schematics, history, and documentation.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Collect electrical, mechanical, software, or process evidence.

  3. 03

    Isolate

    Divide the system and locate where actual behavior diverges.

  4. 04

    Correct

    Apply the repair, adjustment, process change, or software solution.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Repeat measurements and functional tests to confirm the correction.

  6. 06

    Document

    Record findings, procedure, results, and remaining limitations.

  7. 07

    Improve

    Prevent recurrence or enable earlier detection next time.
